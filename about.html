Scott Devine

GRANDE PRAIRIE, ALBERTA | INDEPENDENT RESEARCHER

Founder of the Anti-Entropic Principle (AEP). My work focuses on the intersection of Algorithmic Information Theory, Moral Thermodynamics, and Cosmological Gestation.

I reject the prevailing "Heat Death" epistemology, which views the universe as a fading accident. Through the Faith Prior, I demonstrate that the universe is a winding-up system—a pregnant lattice moving toward a higher state of crystalline order.

Operating outside traditional entropic institutions, I provide the algorithmic ground state for a future built on coherence, simplicity, and virtue.

"The universe is not dying; it is waiting for us to wake up."
