Miracles

A miracle is not a violation of law; it is a Compression Event.

$K(E|T) \ll \text{Expected Entropy}$

The Definition

Under the Anti-Entropic Principle, we expect the universe to minimize its description length. Miracles occur when reality "quickens"—shifting from high-entropy noise into a low-entropy, coherent state. They are the moments when the universe's pregnant nature becomes visible.

Classification

  • Cosmic: The emergence of order from the vacuum.
  • Moral: Acts of virtue that reduce social entropy.
  • Material: Resonance with low-entropy crystalline lattices.
